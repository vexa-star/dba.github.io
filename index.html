What is the difference between a DBA and a PhD?+

Both the Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) and the Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) are terminal doctoral degrees conferring the title of "Doctor". However, while a traditional PhD prepares candidates primarily for full-time academic research and university teaching, the DBA is tailored for experienced executives. It emphasizes applied research, evidence-based management, and solving complex strategic challenges within organizations and industries.

How can I balance the DBA with a demanding executive position in the GCC?+

The DBA is designed specifically for working executives across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, and the GCC. Delivery is modular, featuring weekend seminars in Dubai Knowledge Park, virtual masterclasses, and one-on-one digital supervisory meetings. Candidates are able to continue their full-time executive responsibilities throughout the 3-year journey.

Is the DBA officially accredited in the UAE?+

Yes. The Doctorate in Business Administration (DBA) program at EM Normandie in Dubai is officially licensed and accredited by the UAE Ministry of Education through the Commission for Academic Accreditation (CAA). This ensures complete federal recognition across government bodies, state-owned enterprises, and private multinational corporations.

What is the structure of the DBA dissertation?+

The DBA dissertation is an extensive, practice-oriented research study (typically 40,000 to 60,000 words) addressing a major organizational, industry, or policy challenge. Under the guidance of your assigned doctoral supervisor, you formulate the problem, collect and analyze empirical data, develop actionable strategic frameworks, and defend your work before an academic jury.

What are the payment terms and is corporate sponsorship accepted?+

The program tuition is AED 300,000 over 3 years (AED 120,000 in Year 1, AED 90,000 in Year 2, and AED 90,000 in Year 3). Tuition can be paid via annual or semester installments. Corporate and governmental sponsorship documentation, formal invoices, and tax compliance receipts are provided for sponsored executives.
